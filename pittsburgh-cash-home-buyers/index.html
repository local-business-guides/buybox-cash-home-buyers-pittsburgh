A short checklist before you talk

You don't need to make the house look ready for visitors before reaching out. It can still help to gather a few basics so the conversation stays focused:

  • The property's address and your preferred way to be contacted.
  • A rough idea of when you'd like to sell or move.
  • Any known repairs, maintenance concerns, or areas you want to explain.
  • Questions about belongings, paperwork, or the steps after an offer.
  • Any timing or personal circumstances you want the buyer to consider.

Share only what you're comfortable discussing at first. If other people have an interest in the property or need to be part of the decision, consider including them early so everyone can review the same information.
